### Interpretation

**Rochester publishes the assessment data Ontario denies.** The UK reference
atlas's method rests on VOA rating-list values; MPAC withholds the Ontario
equivalent. Rochester's `CURRENT_LAND_VALUE` against `CURRENT_TOTAL_VALUE`
gives improvement value directly, and **4,717 parcels currently carry zero of
it** — an observation from a register, not an inference from absence. That is a
categorically stronger evidence class than anything the Niagara method can
produce, and twelve snapshots make it a trajectory rather than a snapshot.

**Waterloo repeats the Niagara structural pattern** — one shared catalogue, an
upper tier plus lower-tier municipalities, separate address layers per
municipality on separate hosts. Its ceiling is the `footprint` tier: no parcels.

**Consequence for the engine:** under-occupancy cannot be one computation. In
Ontario it is a ratio of floor area and employment against parcel area, computed
from the employment upper bound. In Rochester it is improvement value over total
value from an assessment register. Same question, different method, different
evidence class — and the class has to travel with the score rather than being
averaged away.

**Not probed, recorded so the gap is explicit:** Region of Waterloo's own 125
datasets; Monroe County (portal does not resolve); licence terms for either
region; Buffalo. And critically, **whether `PARCELID` is genuinely stable across
all twelve Rochester snapshots is assumed, not verified** — it is the single
most load-bearing assumption here and needs the same test `nei_id` got.

## 2 · Enrichment design written

Wrote `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-27-atlas-enrichment-design.md` (413 lines,
14 sections). Design conversation ran through a long discovery phase; every
correction below was checked against real data before being written in.

Access attributes corrected — six of the engine spec's original access
attributes measured the wrong geometry:
```
dist_rail_m       -> dist_rail_served_m      (spur/siding/yard/industrial; exclude crossover, mainline)
dist_regional_road_m -> dist_truck_route_m   (+ RESTRICTED, TIME_OF_DAY)
dist_highway_m    -> dist_interchange_m      (265 motorway junctions observed)
dist_border_m     -> dist_commercial_crossing_m (Rainbow Bridge is passenger-only)
dist_canal_m      -> dist_water_loading_m    (~18 real points; canal is transit not loading)
dist_airport_m    -> dist_cargo_airport_m    (Hamilton J.C. Munro is cargo; Niagara District is not)
transit_400m      -> transit_service_at_shift_times (GTFS)
```

Servicing recovered as capacity rather than membership: Hamilton sewer main
54,115 features carry PIPEDIAM/PIPEHT/INSTDATE; watermain 38,298 carry
WAT_SIZE/PRESZONE/INSTDATE.

Two constraint reclassifications: archaeological potential is a screening
trigger not a prohibition; heritage designated and listed are different legal
states. New veto class for conditions that disqualify rather than subtract —
low clearance (229 maxheight features), attenuation zones (subsurface),
landlocked parcels, non-commercial crossings.

Seven provincial regulatory registers added as a source class: HWIN (annual
2002-), EWRB, Permit to Take Water, Spills (2003-2022), Aggregate Sites,
Waste Management Sites, Attenuation Zones. All OGL Ontario, all address-keyed,
all consumed through the J2 join built in plan 1.

Agri-industrial: 612 greenhouses, 836 vineyards, 176 orchards, 82 wineries
observed in the study area.

### Interpretation

The design's substance is corrections, not additions. Every one of the six
access attributes computed cleanly and answered a question nobody asked — the
same failure that produced 64 false departures in plan 1, and the reason the
discovery phase ran as long as it did.

Two findings changed what the engine can claim rather than merely how it
computes. HWIN beats the NEI on regulatory standing, time depth and scale
measurement. EWRB closes a gap the engine spec had recorded as structural: it
observes occupancy through energy consumption where MPAC denies it through
assessment. The general lesson, written into the design as §8: regulation leaves
better evidence than planning does, and none of these registers appears in any
municipal catalogue.
