# St. Catharines

 St. Catharines is where the Welland Canal climbs out of Lake Ontario.
 Ships, steel and machine shops have shaped the city for generations, and a
 university sits on the escarpment above it. We want to know the people who keep
 its shops running and the students who could join them.

 Aspirational. We have no presence in St. Catharines yet. This
 page says why we want relationships, partnerships and resources there, and what we
 hope to build with them.

 What we have seen

 Two of the manufacturers in START Sparks, our record of
 investments across the corridor from July to September 2026, are in St. Catharines:

 CompanyWhat was reported

 FBT Innovative Technologies
 A $9.54 million facility expansion and new metalworking machinery, announced 25 September 2026, creating 20 jobs. Part of a provincial announcement covering four Niagara manufacturers.
 Canada Perforating
 Federal support for advanced machinery. Described in our Ontario Corridor Brief as Canada’s only full-service perforator, serving nuclear, architectural and industrial clients.

 The same brief notes that Ontario announced additional postsecondary seats at
 Brock University and Niagara College, including STEM and trades seats, in the same
 window as the investments.

 What we would like to build

 Visits to working shops. Tours of
 fabricators and machine shops, for members, students and the curious.
 A bridge to the campus. Projects and placements that put students next to
 a bench and the people who run one.
 Training close to the work. Short courses shaped by what local shops
 say they need, alongside our training plans.
 Space. Conversations about benches, bays and storage near the canal and
 the highway.

 Help shape it

 If you run a shop in St. Catharines, teach or study nearby, or own a building that
 could host a bench, we would like to hear from you. See also
 Niagara Falls and Fort Erie
 along the river, and Hamilton, where we begin.
