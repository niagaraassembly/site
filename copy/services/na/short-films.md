# Short Films

Written 2026-10-13 for the new NA* division page. Live in `services/na/short-films/index.html`.

Short documentary films, from a few minutes to a quarter of an hour. Longer than an edit and slower: time enough to follow a part through a shop, sit with a founder deciding whether to expand, or watch a town weigh what a new plant would mean for its housing and its schools.

Short films are where NA* can take on the harder questions about growth and adaptation: what changes when a sector arrives or leaves, and who gets to decide. Have a story from your shop, your street or your field? Tell us about it.
