# Exhibits

Written 2026-10-13 for the new NA* division page. Live in `services/na/exhibits/index.html`.

Exhibits and events that bring industry into a room. Photographs, film, finished products, samples and the tools that made them, shown where people already gather: a gallery, a hall, a storefront, a shop floor opened for an evening.

An exhibit is a chance to meet in person, ask questions and argue about what a region should make next. We want to stage them with artists, curators, venues and the companies whose work is on show. Have a space, a collection or an idea for a show? Write to us.
