An open home for hardware.

Sept 26, 2026  ·  ~1 min read

This post is brief on purpose. We want to draft a motivating “call for participation” that can be adopted by us or by anyone who wants to create places where hardware education and development can take deep root.

We think the title spells out the three main pillars: hardware focused, a true home, and an open attitude toward education and innovation. We’ll soon publish a draft which will simply be raw material to iterate upon.

If you need convincing that the moment is real, the industrial activity across the Niagara–Buffalo–Rochester corridor in just the past two months speaks for itself.

Please join us on this journey and let’s manufacture something amazing.

To discuss these ideas, propose a collaboration, or simply say hello — reach out at office@niagaraassembly.com, or find us on X, Discord, and LinkedIn.
