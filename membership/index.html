Membership

@NiagaraAssembly is a members-led company. Its purpose is to help Members pioneer the work spaces and digital tools that lift all boats across the Niagara Peninsula — from Brantford and Port Dover through Hamilton, east across Haldimand to Fort Erie and Niagara Falls, and over the river into Erie and Niagara counties.

We are not a fund and we are not a chamber of commerce. We are a lightweight, soon to be incorporated collective that connects industrial operators and entrepreneurs through common spaces, sponsored stand-ups, special events, certified training, and peer-led workshops, and we build the tools that make all those connections useful between meetings. We are a place to find other builders and where building happens, from small electronics solutions for business, to robotics, and beyond.

We want our spaces, our events, and our programming to be the place where locals find the future and we need your ideas and your assistance in building communities.

What we are building

As the Membership grows and self-organizing takes hold, we are testing product and service ideas that can make the company profitable and have major impact across the region and beyond.

  • Mobile electronics specialist training, delivered where the work is rather than where a classroom happens to be.
  • Real estate and logistics visualization systems — the Industrial Atlas is the first of these, and it is public.
  • Integrated Production Environments using Theia.

Membership

Membership is how the company is governed and how it is funded. There are three levels beyond the mailing list.

  • Regional — for individuals working in or around industry in the region.
  • Company — for firms that want their people connected to the network and their capacity visible on the board.
  • Champion — for those backing the work beyond their own immediate return.

Nothing is charged through this site. Expressing interest starts a conversation, and someone will be in touch. Put your name down here.
