Board

The Board is a public bulletin board for the region. Anyone can post what is happening, what they have, and what they are looking for, and anyone can browse it.

Open the Board   Post to the Board

Six sections

SectionWhat goes thereKinds
EventsSomething happening on a date, at a place. Standups, Talks, Demos, Launches, Workshops, Training
NewsA link and a few lines about what changed. New Projects, New Companies, Hiring, Expansions, SAFEs, Other Investment
SpacesSpace someone else could use, roughly what and on what terms. Events, Office Space, Industrial, Retail, Yard, Warehouse
ToolsA machine or tool others could get at. Electronics, Fabrication, Manufacturing, Warehouse, Other
ExpertsPeople offering training, workshops or their skills. Software, Electronics, Fabrication, Manufacturing, Logistics, Management, Other
ShowsA personal or shared practice, narrated over time. Shops, Factories, Makerspaces, Studios, Labs

Offering and seeking

For Spaces, Tools and Experts, a post can be an offer or a request. A board that can only say “I have” is half a board, so you can also say “I need”. Events and news are neither offered nor sought, so they carry no such label.

How it works

  • Post. Fill in the short form. Each section asks only for what it needs.
  • Review. Posts appear on the Board once they have been reviewed.
  • Privacy. Your name and email are used for follow-up and are never published. You choose the public contact that appears on the post.
  • Browse. Filter by section, kind and town, by whether it is offering or seeking, and by time: past week, past month, or upcoming only.

Experts can choose whether a post is published, kept as a private registration with Niagara Assembly, or both.
